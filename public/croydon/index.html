(01) The record

It started here

Croydon holds three generations of Black British cultural life in one family story. Samuel Coleridge-Taylor, the borough's internationally celebrated composer; his widow Jessie, who sought out a Whitgift schoolboy — a distant cousin through Sierra Leonean kinship networks — and claimed him into the family; and that boy, Ivor Cummings of Addiscombe, who grew up to be the openly gay civil servant welcoming the Windrush generation ashore at Tilbury in 1948. The name over every BLKOUT door belongs to a Croydon son, claimed by a Croydon family.

The line continues: David McAlmont, one of the great British voices, is Croydon's too. The Museum of Croydon's BlackOut exhibition keeps the borough's Black queer story on record, and BLKOUT's own heritage project, Ivor's Compass, is part of the borough's Coleridge-Taylor 150 year. This door is that story in the present tense.

The 2021 Census counted around 88,500 Black residents in Croydon — the largest Black population of any London borough, which on any reasonable estimate makes Croydon home to more Black queer men than anywhere else in the capital. The same census counted over 9,000 Croydon adults who identify as LGB+ — and more than 28,000 who preferred not to answer the question at all. The closet has a census line. These are working estimates and we welcome correction — but the point stands: we were never a niche. We were just never counted.

(05) Health

Look after yourself

Free, confidential, no GP needed, no judgement — and your business stays yours.

  • Croydon Sexual Health

    The borough's free NHS clinic — STI testing and treatment, contraception, PrEP — and free self-test kits posted to any Croydon address. 530 London Road, CR7 7YE. (Croydon runs its own service; the London-wide SHL scheme doesn't cover this borough.)

    checked
  • 56 Dean Street

    Soho's LGBTQ+ specialist sexual health and HIV service — Europe's largest, and long the home clinic of queer London.

    checked
  • The Love Tank

    Community health action by and for us — the team behind PrEPster, directed by Marc Thompson of the Black and Gay, Back in the Day archive.

(11) Questions

Asked, answered

Where can I meet other Black queer men in Croydon?

Start with the door on this page — you'll hear about everything as it happens. Stanley Arts carries much of the borough's queer life including Ballroom, and BLKOUT's national community is open the moment you knock.

Is there a Black queer night in Croydon?

Stanley Arts in South Norwood hosts Ballroom — Black queer performance culture, in the borough. As more starts, it appears here first.

Where can I get an STI test or PrEP in Croydon?

Croydon Sexual Health at 530 London Road is free and confidential with PrEP available — and posts free self-test kits to any Croydon address. 56 Dean Street is thirty minutes up the line. No GP referral, no judgement.

Is BLKOUT a Croydon organisation?

BLKOUT is one community-owned co-operative for Black queer men across the UK — with a door in every borough. We're registered in Croydon, the borough our namesake grew up in, and we belong wherever our men are.

I'm not out. Can I still come?

Yes. Come as you are — nobody at this door asks you to be anything first, and your business stays yours.

(13) The members' room

Step inside

The door is how we find each other. BLKOUTHUB is where we live.

The members' room: Black queer men across the UK, owned by the men in it. Why knock:

  • Real connection. Nearly four in five members say they came to find it — and found it.
  • No algorithm, no ads, nothing sold onward. A room that doesn't monetise you. Your attention is yours; we're after your company.
  • Where things actually start. Occasions, groups, and the knowledge that never gets written down — passed man to man, the way it always has been.
  • Owned by us. A co-operative with an asset lock: what we build together stays ours, and passes to the men who come after.

(15) For professionals

If you work here

This page is for Black queer men first. But if you serve this borough — NHS, social prescribing, council, community sector, creative health, or you run a room — there's a door for you too.

  • Refer. Supporting a Black queer man who could use his people? Send him this page — that is a referral, and every knock gets answered by a human.
  • Commission or partner. Working on Black queer men's health, culture or connection in Croydon? We're the community-owned, by-and-for partner — and the evidence base is already on this page. Write to rob@blkoutuk.com.
  • List us. Maintain a directory, a support page, a signposting service? Our entry, ready to paste: BLKOUT — the UK's community-owned co-operative for Black queer men. In your neighbourhood: https://events.blkoutuk.com/croydon/ · blkoutuk.com · rob@blkoutuk.com
  • Run a room. A venue, night or group our men should know about? Tell us — the page gets truer, and it points at you.
